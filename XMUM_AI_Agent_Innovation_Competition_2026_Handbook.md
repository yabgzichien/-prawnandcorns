## About the Competition

Have you ever encountered a real-world problem or a process that could be made easier, faster or more efficient with AI?

The **XMUM AI Agent Innovation Competition 2026** invites you to turn these ideas into practical AI-powered solutions.

Organised by the Innovation and Entrepreneurship Centre (IEC), this competition challenges students to identify real-world problems, operational needs and opportunities for improvement across various environments, and develop customised AI Agent solutions to address them.

**Participants are encouraged to explore AI Agent applications in areas such as business operations, workplace productivity, education, information management, customer service, administrative workflows and other relevant fields.**

Whether addressing challenges within the university, in external organisations, during internships or in everyday life, participants have the opportunity to experiment with AI technologies, develop functional prototypes and demonstrate how their ideas can create meaningful real-world impact.

---

## Competition Eligibility & Participation

1. **Eligible Participants** — All XMUM undergraduate and postgraduate students
2. **Participation Mode** — Participate individually or in teams, 1–6 members per project
3. **Project Limit** — Each participant may join only one project
4. **Submission Language** — All proposals, presentation slides and demonstrations must be in English
5. **Project Originality** — All projects must be original and developed by the participating individual or team
6. **Mandatory On-site Attendance** — At least one to two representatives from each team must attend the onsite competition at XMUM. The team leader is required to be present
7. **Pitching Duration** — 10-minute presentation, followed by a 5-minute Q&A session
8. **Development Platforms** — Participants may freely choose their preferred AI Agent platforms, tools and models. All tools and platforms used must be disclosed

---

## Project Submission Requirements

Participants are required to submit the following materials online:

1. **Presentation Slides** — Project concept, problem statement and proposed solution.
2. **AI Agent Prototype** — A functional AI Agent or Minimum Viable Product (MVP).
3. **Project Proposal in Word (Optional)** — A written explanation of the project and its expected benefits.

- Submission Deadline: **16 December 2026**
- All submission materials must be prepared in **English**.
- **No amendments, modifications or resubmissions** will be permitted once the submission has been completed.
- **All on-site presentations must be delivered in English.**

## Reimbursement Criteria

**MAXIMUM REIMBURSEMENT: RM150 PER TEAM**

- Only competition-related AI API expenses incurred during the competition period are eligible.
- Only one reimbursement claim is allowed per participating team.
- The project submission must be completed and verified by the Organising Committee.
- Claims must be supported by an official receipt/invoice and proof of payment.
- Claims must be submitted to IEC by **29 December 2026**.
- All claims are subject to IEC verification and Finance Department approval.

**Required Documents for Reimbursement:**

1. **Completed Student Claim Form** — To be completed by the team leader and signed by all team members.
2. **Official Receipt(s) / Invoice(s)** — Evidence of the AI API expenses incurred.
3. **Proof of Payment** — Payment transaction records or other supporting evidence confirming that the payment has been made.
4. **API Usage Record** — A usage report or screenshot from the API provider's dashboard showing the actual API consumption, including usage details and corresponding costs, where available.

**Submission Method:** All reimbursement documents must be submitted via email to **IEC at innovation@xmu.edu.my**.

## Evaluation Criteria

### 1. Problem Identification and Relevance (20%)

| Sub-criterion | Detailed Evaluation Indicators | Weight (Marks) |
|---|---|---|
| Problem Definition | Clearly identifies a specific, focused and understandable problem, challenge or unmet need. The problem is sufficiently bounded and not overly broad or vague. | 8% |
| Target User and Needs Analysis | Identifies the intended users or beneficiaries and demonstrates an understanding of their needs, pain points, behaviours and usage context. | 6% |
| Problem Significance | Explains why the identified problem deserves attention, including its current consequences, urgency or importance to the target users or context. | 6% |

### 2. Innovation and Creativity (20%)

| Sub-criterion | Detailed Evaluation Indicators | Weight (Marks) |
|---|---|---|
| Originality and Novelty | Demonstrates an original concept, approach or meaningful adaptation. Clearly identifies what is new or distinctive compared with existing solutions; does not rely solely on claims of being innovative. | 10% |
| Innovative Application of AI Agent Capabilities | Selects and applies AI Agent capabilities in a creative and problem-relevant way. The use of agent-based methods contributes meaningfully to the proposed solution rather than being included for appearance. | 10% |

### 3. Functionality and Technical Implementation (25%)

| Sub-criterion | Detailed Evaluation Indicators | Weight (Marks) |
|---|---|---|
| Agent Task Execution and Autonomy | Demonstrates the ability to interpret a task, plan or follow appropriate steps, make contextually relevant decisions and execute actions. The degree of autonomy and human oversight is appropriate to the use case. | 8% |
| Technical Architecture and Integration | Uses a coherent and appropriate technical design, including AI models, APIs, external tools, databases, knowledge sources or other components where relevant. The team can explain how the components work together. | 5% |
| Accuracy and Reliability | Produces relevant and sufficiently consistent outputs, handles user inputs appropriately and manages errors, uncertainty or unexpected behaviour in a way suitable for the intended use. | 5% |
| Prototype Completeness | Presents a sufficiently developed and operational prototype. Core features and the intended end-to-end workflow are implemented to a level appropriate for a competition prototype. | 4% |
| Testing and Validation | Provides appropriate evidence that the prototype has been tested, such as test cases, sample scenarios, user feedback, iteration or checks of output quality and system behaviour. | 3% |

### 4. Practical Application and Potential Impact (20%)

| Sub-criterion | Detailed Evaluation Indicators | Weight (Marks) |
|---|---|---|
| Implementation Feasibility | Shows that the solution can realistically be deployed or piloted in its intended environment, taking account of relevant operational, technical, resource, data or user constraints. | 6% |
| Practical Usefulness and Value | Explains how the solution addresses user needs and improves a task, service or process, with plausible direct benefits such as time savings, accessibility, quality or efficiency. | 6% |
| Scalability and Sustainability | Considers the potential to extend the solution to more users, tasks or contexts and maintain it over time, including relevant maintenance, operating or resource requirements. | 4% |
| Broader Potential Impact | Identifies plausible wider benefits or implications for organisations, communities, industries or other stakeholders beyond the immediate user-level benefit. | 4% |

### 5. Presentation and Demonstration (15%)

| Sub-criterion | Detailed Evaluation Indicators | Weight (Marks) |
|---|---|---|
| Presentation Clarity and Structure | Communicates the problem, target users, proposed solution, key features and technical approach in a logical, clear and well-organised manner. | 5% |
| Live Demonstration Effectiveness | Shows the actual system workflow and key functions clearly and efficiently, enabling judges to understand what the prototype does and how it addresses the problem. | 5% |
| Response to Judges' Questions | Responds clearly, accurately and thoughtfully to questions about the problem, design decisions, implementation, limitations, testing and potential applications. | 5% |

**FINAL SCORE: 100%**
